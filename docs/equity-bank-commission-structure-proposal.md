# Betech Solar and Equity Bank Referral Commission Structure

**Prepared for:** Equity Bank Kenya Limited  
**Prepared by:** Betech Solar Solutions  
**Date:** 29 October 2026  
**Status:** Proposal for discussion

## Purpose

Betech Solar Solutions proposes an inclusive referral programme through which Equity Bank employees, WIG participants, students and other eligible individuals may introduce customers to Betech for solar products, systems and installation services. The programme is designed to create a transparent, auditable and customer-centred route from referral to completed sale.

Betech will manage product advice, quotations, site assessment, fulfilment, installation, payment follow-up, customer support and warranty support. Each eligible individual referrer earns commission only for qualifying customers attributed to them through the agreed referral process.

## Proposed commission model

| Item | Proposed structure |
| --- | --- |
| Commission rate | **6%** of the qualifying completed sale value, unless a specific product or campaign has a separately approved commission amount. |
| Eligible transactions | Solar products, solar kits, batteries, inverters, pumps and installation projects sold by Betech to a customer validly referred by Equity Bank. |
| Commission basis | The final Betech-approved sale value actually paid by the customer, after approved discounts and excluding cancelled, refunded or reversed items. Delivery, installation and other charges are included only where they form part of the approved sale value. |
| Referral attribution | A unique Equity Bank referral code, link, lead form or other agreed identifier must be captured before, or at the time, the customer order is submitted. |
| Commission status | The referral is recorded as **pending** at submission, remains **locked** while fulfilment is in progress, and becomes **earned** only after payment and completion are confirmed. |
| Payment method | Settlement to the eligible referrer's nominated M-Pesa wallet, bank account or another mutually agreed payment channel, supported by a reconciliation statement. |

### Illustrative examples

| Customer purchase value | Proposed commission at 6% |
| ---: | ---: |
| KES 50,000 | KES 3,000 |
| KES 280,000 | KES 16,800 |
| KES 950,000 | KES 57,000 |

The examples are illustrative. The payable amount is calculated from the final qualifying sale value and not from a quotation, deposit, unconfirmed order or cancelled transaction.

## Customer and order journey

1. An eligible Equity Bank employee, WIG participant, student or other approved referrer introduces a customer through the agreed referral channel.
2. Betech records the referral attribution and confirms receipt of the lead or order.
3. Betech advises the customer, prepares a quotation where needed, and carries out any site assessment required for a system design.
4. Betech fulfils the order and confirms the customer's payment and successful delivery, collection or installation completion.
5. The commission changes from locked to earned and appears in the partner reconciliation statement.
6. Betech settles approved earned commissions in the agreed payment cycle.

## Eligibility and controls

- A referral qualifies only when the customer has not already been attributed to another Betech agent, partner, campaign or direct sales channel.
- The referral identifier must be captured accurately and be traceable in Betech's order records.
- Commission is not payable on cancelled orders, refunds, chargebacks, fraudulent transactions, unpaid balances or transactions that fail Betech's fulfilment and payment-confirmation checks.
- If a qualifying sale is later refunded, reversed or materially reduced, Betech may reverse or adjust the related commission in the next reconciliation cycle. Any adjustment will be shown clearly in the statement.
- Betech may set a fixed commission amount, rather than 6%, for particular products or campaigns where this is communicated and approved in advance.
- Equity Bank and Betech will agree a process for resolving duplicate attribution, customer disputes and exceptions before programme launch.

## Visibility, reporting and reconciliation

Betech will provide a periodic reconciliation statement showing, at minimum:

- referral reference and date received;
- customer reference, with personal data limited to what is necessary and lawful to share;
- product or project category;
- sale value used for calculation;
- applicable commission rate or fixed amount;
- commission status: pending, locked, earned, paid, adjusted or reversed;
- payment or settlement reference; and
- reason for any adjustment, rejection or reversal.

The programme can use tracked links, referral codes or approved lead forms to preserve attribution. Commission information remains internal to Betech and Equity Bank; it is not displayed to customers as part of the public product experience.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Eligible referrers | Betech Solar Solutions |
| --- | --- |
| Introduce customers through approved channels. | Provide current product, pricing and programme information. |
| Use only approved communications and referral identifiers. | Capture and protect referral attribution. |
| Avoid making unapproved technical, credit, warranty or delivery promises. | Handle technical advice, quotations, site assessment, fulfilment, installation and customer support. |
| Provide required settlement details and support reconciliation queries. | Confirm payment and completion, calculate commissions and issue reconciliation statements. |

## Governance and compliance

The parties will document the final commercial agreement, including payment cycle, tax treatment, data-protection obligations, branding approval, customer-consent requirements, audit rights, dispute handling, termination and notice provisions. This proposal does not create an obligation to pay commission until those terms have been approved by both parties in writing.

Customer personal data will be shared only where necessary for the referral, order fulfilment, customer support and reconciliation process, and in accordance with applicable Kenyan law and each party's privacy obligations.

## Proposed next steps

2. Agree the referral-attribution method and the treatment of duplicate leads.
3. Confirm whether the standard 6% model applies to all eligible categories or whether product-specific rates are required.
4. Agree the settlement frequency, invoicing requirements, tax treatment and bank-account verification process.
5. Finalise a pilot scope, reporting format, customer communication approvals and operational contacts.
